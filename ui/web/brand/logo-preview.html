WRAITH — Brand-Mark Concepts

Three original spectral-wraith concepts. Each shown as the full mark and the simplified compact glyph, at 16 / 28 / 64 / 128px on dark (left) and light (right) panels. Marks use currentColor (shown here in accent cyan) + layered opacity — no hard-coded hex, theme-safe. Judge the 16px read especially.

Concept B — SHROUD (hooded cowl, void face) RECOMMENDED

A draped spectral hood with a hollow cut-out face and two bright floating eyes; a three-tongue flame rises behind the peak; shoulders fray at the base. Reads instantly as a hooded phantom; the hollow-void construction gives a refined, premium logo feel that reduces cleanly.
